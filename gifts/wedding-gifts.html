Gift guide

Wedding photos deserve more than a folder of selects.

A wedding book can be a keepsake for the couple, a gift for parents, or a way to preserve the full story after the event is over. The right format gives those images weight.

Where it fits best

  • Wedding albums for couples
  • Parent thank-you gifts
  • Anniversary keepsakes
  • Post-wedding family books

Suggested format

Wedding Keepsake with more pages for ceremony, portraits, family, and celebrations.
