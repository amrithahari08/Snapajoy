Gift guide

A Diwali gift that feels personal, not replaceable.

When families search for Diwali gift ideas, they are often deciding between something temporary and something meaningful. A family photo book gives them the second option.

Why it fits Diwali

  • Feels thoughtful without being overly formal
  • Works for parents, grandparents, and close family
  • Turns the year's family photos into one gift-ready object

Suggested format

Family yearbook or festival memory book with warm, gift-led sequencing.
