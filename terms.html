Terms

How order approval and site use are understood.

These terms explain what counts as approval, how the ordering flow works, and what customers should understand before print begins.

Order approval

Printing begins only after the customer reviews and approves the draft shared through the active order flow.

Customer-supplied content

The customer is responsible for sending photos they have the right to share and print.

Order changes

Changes are handled before approval. Once a book is approved for print, further changes may not be possible.

Support and policies

Privacy, delivery, and replacement handling should be read together with the linked support pages.
