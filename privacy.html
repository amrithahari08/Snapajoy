Privacy and photo handling

Your photos are used to make your photo book

Snapajoy uses the photos and order details you share to design, print, deliver, and support your photo book.

Photo book creation

Snapajoy uses your photos to check print quality, prepare layouts, create previews, make approved edits, and produce your printed photo book.

Marketing consent

Snapajoy does not use your photos for marketing without your consent.

Storage and support

Photos and order details may be stored while your design, print, delivery, and support requests remain active.

Delete your photos

You can request deletion anytime. Type help in the WhatsApp chat and follow the support prompts.

Have a privacy question?

Send your question on WhatsApp so the team can respond in the same conversation as your order.
