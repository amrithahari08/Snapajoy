Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 8, 2026

Snapajoy is designed to make creating photo albums simple and safe. This page explains how we handle your photos, messages, and order details when you use Snapajoy. If you have questions, you can always contact us through WhatsApp support.

1. Photos you send

When you create an album with Snapajoy, you send photos through WhatsApp.

These photos are used only for the purpose of creating your photo album preview and final printed album.

We do not sell your photos or use them for advertising.

Your photos remain private and are only accessed by the systems and team members needed to prepare your album.

2. Album preview generation

After you send photos, Snapajoy creates an album preview.

You can review and request edits before the album is printed.

Printing begins only after you approve the preview.

This step helps ensure that your album looks exactly the way you want.

3. Information we collect

To process your order, we may collect the following information:

  • photos you upload
  • album selections and layout choices
  • your WhatsApp contact information
  • delivery address
  • order details and payment confirmation

This information is used only to create and deliver your album and provide customer support.

4. How we store your photos

Photos and album files may be temporarily stored while your album is being created and delivered.

Access is limited to the systems and team members required to prepare your album.

We use secure service providers to store and process files when necessary.

5. How long photos are stored

Uploaded photos and album files may be stored for a limited period to:

  • complete album design
  • process printing
  • provide support if changes are requested
  • resolve delivery or quality issues

After this period, files may be deleted or removed from active systems.

6. Third-party services

To operate Snapajoy, we may use trusted third-party services for:

  • cloud storage
  • printing services
  • payment processing
  • delivery and shipping

These providers only receive the information needed to perform their role.

7. WhatsApp communication

Snapajoy uses WhatsApp to communicate with customers and receive photos.

Messages and media shared through WhatsApp are used only for creating and managing your album order.

8. Your choices

You can request the deletion of your photos or album files by contacting Snapajoy support.

We will review and process deletion requests where possible.

Photo Storage & Handling

When you create an album with Snapajoy, your photos may be temporarily stored to generate previews and complete the printing process.

Files may be stored while:

  • creating your album design
  • processing printing
  • handling delivery or support requests

Access to photos is limited to the systems and team members required to prepare your album.

Snapajoy may use trusted service providers for storage and printing. These providers only receive the information necessary to complete your order.

After the order process is completed and the support window passes, files may be removed from active systems.

Can I delete my photos?

Yes.

If you want your uploaded photos or album files removed from Snapajoy systems, you can request deletion through customer support.

We will review and process deletion requests where possible.

Some order records may need to be retained for operational or legal purposes.

9. Policy updates

We may update this privacy policy if our services or legal obligations change.

When updates are made, the latest version will always appear on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about privacy or data handling, please contact Snapajoy support.

We will do our best to help.
