Stories and proof

Trust works best when it is visible.

Snapajoy should earn trust through approved customer stories, printed product visuals, and a clear preview-before-print flow, not through exaggerated claims.

Stories need permission

Customer names, images, and quotes should appear only when the family or couple agrees to share them publicly.

Ratings need evidence

Public review language should be backed by real sources and real customer feedback.

Product visuals do trust work

Hands-on product shots, layout previews, and clear expectations help more than generic five-star icons.

What real proof can show

When stories are published, they should feel grounded.

Occasion context

Why the book was ordered: a parent birthday, anniversary, wedding, baby milestone, or family yearbook.

Visual proof

Images of the printed result, not just a quote. Printed pages and cover photography matter.

Order clarity

What the buyer valued most: preview-before-print, gift impact, ease of ordering, or product quality.
