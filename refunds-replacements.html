Refunds & Replacements

Snapajoy prints albums only after you approve the preview. This helps reduce mistakes and ensures the album matches your expectations.

1. Preview approval

Before printing begins, you will receive an album preview.

Please review the preview carefully and confirm when you are satisfied.

Once printing starts, changes may not be possible.

2. Damaged or defective albums

If your album arrives damaged or has a printing defect, please contact Snapajoy support.

We may ask for photos of the issue so we can review it.

If a production or delivery issue is confirmed, we may offer a replacement.

3. Incorrect information

If an album is printed based on incorrect photos or information provided during the order process, replacements may not always be possible.

4. Order cancellation

Orders may be cancelled before printing begins.

Once printing starts, cancellation may not be possible.

5. Support

If you experience any issue with your album, please contact Snapajoy.

We will do our best to help resolve the problem.
