How Snapajoy works

Send your photos, shape the design, and approve your photo book before printing begins.

  1. 01

    Send your photos on WhatsApp

    Share the moments you want in your book. Your original photos can come from your phone, camera, cloud folder, or family collection.

  2. 02

    We check photo quality

    Snapajoy checks the photos and recommends a print size that suits their quality.

  3. 03

    Choose size and pages

    Choose 8 x 6, 8 x 8, or 10 x 10 in. You can change the size and page count before approval.

  4. 04

    Review a free video preview

    See the complete photo book story before you place the order.

  5. 05

    Ask for free edits

    Tell us what you want changed on WhatsApp. Edits are free before approval.

  6. 06

    Fine-tune on the web

    Open the web editor when you want advanced layout, crop, caption, cover, or page changes.

    Open the web editor
  7. 07

    Approve and place the order

    Printing begins after you are happy with the design. Your book ships in about 1 week after approval.
