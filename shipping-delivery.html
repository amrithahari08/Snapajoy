Shipping & Delivery

Snapajoy albums are printed after you approve your album preview. This ensures your album looks exactly the way you want before printing begins.

1. Printing process

Once your preview is approved, the album enters the printing process.

Production time depends on the album type and printing queue.

2. Delivery timeline

After printing is complete, your album is packed and shipped to your delivery address.

Delivery times may vary depending on:

  • your location
  • courier service availability
  • public holidays or peak seasons

Tracking information may be shared once the album is shipped.

3. Delivery coverage

Snapajoy aims to deliver albums across most serviceable locations in India.

Some remote areas may have longer delivery timelines depending on courier availability.

4. Address accuracy

Please make sure the delivery address shared during the order process is correct.

Incorrect addresses may cause delivery delays.

5. Delivery issues

If your album arrives damaged or there is a delivery issue, please contact Snapajoy support.

We will review the issue and help resolve it as quickly as possible.

6. Questions

If you need help checking your order status, you can contact Snapajoy through WhatsApp support.
