Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need before placing your first order.

How does Snapajoy work?

Creating an album with Snapajoy is simple. Start on WhatsApp, send your photos, receive an album preview, request edits if needed, approve the preview, and your album is printed and delivered.

Do I get to see the album before printing?

Yes. Snapajoy always shares a preview before printing begins. You can review the layout and request changes before approving the album.

Are my photos private?

Your photos are used only to create your album. They are not sold or used for advertising. Access is limited to the systems and team members required to prepare your album.

Can I edit the album layout?

Yes. When you receive the preview, you can request changes. Printing starts only after you approve the preview.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on printing time and your location. After printing is completed, the album is shipped using a courier service.

What happens if my album arrives damaged?

If your album arrives damaged, contact Snapajoy support. We may request photos of the issue so we can review it and help resolve the problem.

How do I contact support?

You can contact Snapajoy through WhatsApp support. A team member will assist you with your order.

Can I request deletion of my photos?

Yes. You can contact Snapajoy support to request deletion of your uploaded photos or album files.

What kind of albums does Snapajoy create?

Snapajoy creates printed photo albums designed from your photos. Albums may vary in size, paper quality, and binding depending on the product you choose.
