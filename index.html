FAQ

Common photo book questions

Do I pay before seeing the design?

No. You can review the complete design before you order.

Which size should I choose?

Choose 8 x 8 in for most family, travel, and memory books. Choose 8 x 6 in for compact stories and 10 x 10 in for weddings and larger keepsakes.

Can Snapajoy use my photos for marketing?

No. Snapajoy does not use your photos for marketing without your consent.

What happens after I approve the design?

Snapajoy prints your photo book, ships it in about 1 week, and delivers it across India within 2 weeks after approval.
